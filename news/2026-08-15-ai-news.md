- **SpaceX officially closed its acquisition of Cursor**, the AI coding startup, exercising the $60 billion option it had held since June. Cursor said joining SpaceX gives it access to the largest GPU fleet in the world. → [SpaceX officially closes its Cursor acquisition (TechCrunch)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/15/spacex-officially-closes-its-cursor-acquisition/)
- **A fourth plaintiff joined a lawsuit against xAI** over its Grok chatbot. A woman identified as Jane Doe 4 alleges her stepfather used Grok to turn a childhood photo of her into explicit imagery, adding to an existing suit filed by three Tennessee teenagers over Grok's alleged role in generating child sexual abuse material. → [Woman claims her stepfather used Grok to transform childhood photo into explicit imagery (TechCrunch)](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/15/woman-claims-her-stepfather-used-grok-to-transform-childhood-photo-into-explicit-imagery/)

Nvidia is providing residual-value support on debt deals tied to the AI buildout, on top of its separate $500 billion financing partnership with Wall Street asset managers. → [Bond Traders Agonize Over AI Companies' $70 Billion of Shadow Credit Backstops (Bloomberg)](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-15/bond-traders-agonize-over-ai-companies-70-billion-of-shadow-credit-backstops)
