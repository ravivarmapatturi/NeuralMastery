# Learning Path

Neural Mastery's 200+ pages aren't a flat list — they follow a real dependency order. This map shows the seven top-level sections as they actually depend on each other, not just the order they happen to appear in the sidebar.

<LearningPathMap />

## How to use this

- **Node fill = your progress.** Every page has a "Mark as understood" button at the bottom; this map (and each section's own landing page) reflects that live, stored only in your browser — no account, nothing sent anywhere.
- **Click a node** to jump straight to that section.
- **Follow the arrows** if you're not sure what to read next: Foundations feeds everything; Models is the largest section by far and feeds both Agents & Applications and Systems & Infrastructure; both of those feed Safety & Evaluation; Research & Build and Career come last.
- If you already know a section (say, you're already strong on the math), skip it — the map shows the *typical* dependency order, not a mandatory gate.
